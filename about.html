A little about me

  • My journey in technology has been shaped by my experiences, my education, and my ambition to create opportunities for myself and others. I was born in Pakistan as a refugee from Afghanistan and spent many years there before coming to the United States to continue my education. Moving to a new country and starting a new chapter of my life was challenging, but it also gave me the opportunity to pursue goals that were not always possible for me before.
  • I am currently a Computer Science student at the University of Delaware, with a concentration and minor in cybersecurity. Throughout my time at university, I have become especially interested in cybersecurity, digital forensics, machine learning, and security research.
  • Coming from a place where many girls are still denied the opportunity to receive an education has strongly shaped my ambitions. I want to challenge myself by building a career in a field where women are still underrepresented and show through my own experience that gender should never determine someone's ability to learn, succeed, or contribute to technology.
  • One of my long-term goals is to use what I learn and the opportunities I receive to help create more opportunities for girls, especially those who may not have the same access to education that I have had. I want my journey in technology to be an example that education and ambition should not have limits based on gender or where someone comes from.
